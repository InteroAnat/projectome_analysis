Warped template length is not native axon length, and includes both passage fibers and arbors.

Within each evidence stratum and source hemisphere, group maps average contributing animals equally; missing animal/stratum combinations are not inserted as zeros. Additional neurons do not become independent animals. All maps are unsmoothed descriptive outputs; no t-map, p-value or biological connectivity inference is delivered.

The background is the **symmetric population T1-weighted NMT v2.1 MRI**, 256 × 312 × 200 at 0.25 mm, rather than a subject fMOST image. Final [main figures](../figures/multimonkey_coarse_20261009_layout-v2/README.md) and [candidate previews](../figures/additional_candidates_20261009/README.md) use matching individual MRI/map slices at common XYZ voxel indices **56, 200, 87**, with shared contrast within each figure family. This removes the white, flattened depth-MIP background. Density colors show `log10(1 + density)`; occupancy is linear 0–1. Color limits are recorded per family and may differ between runs. These selected cuts do not establish full-volume absence. The sagittal cut is left-sided; right-source maps are not mirrored.
