## Clustering confidence

**Agent: Codex | Date: 2026-10-02 (Asia/Shanghai)**

The user identified clustering as a key uncertainty. Earlier Python settings combined profile-Spearman/log1p distances, a type penalty, Ward linkage and a fixed final `k=30`; the R analysis path used a fixed `k=9`. Those choices did not independently establish a subtype count. Gou's deposited macaque PFC code also uses a type penalty and fixed cut, so guided taxonomy is a legitimate comparison mode; agreement with labels used in the penalty is circular as an enrichment validation.

The revised Python entrypoint defaults to raw FNT dissimilarity, average linkage and no type penalty. It checks complete pair coverage and exact joined-FNT identity, refuses unsupported Ward geometry, exports all candidate cuts and records requested/realized k, hashes and software versions. Spearman explicitly means similarity of **distance-to-cohort profiles**. Its subsampling diagnostics hold that full-cohort representation fixed.

Fresh diagnostic runs used average linkage, k=2..20, 100 subsamples of 80% of neurons and seed 42. These runs measure conditional clustering robustness on historical inputs; they do not validate new cell types.

| Historical 306-neuron representation | Diagnostic/explicit cut | Cluster sizes at k=2 | Silhouette at k=2 | Mean subset ARI at k=2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw FNT, unguided | silhouette suggestion 2 | 3 / 303 | 0.749 | 0.512 |
| log1p FNT, unguided | silhouette suggestion 2 | 1 / 305 | 0.098 | 0.860 |
| Spearman distance profiles, unguided | silhouette suggestion 2 | 147 / 159 | 0.742 | 0.959 |

Raw FNT's high silhouette accompanies a very small high-dissimilarity group; whether these neurons are biological outliers or reflect tracing/measurement differences requires inspection. Profile-Spearman produces a more balanced and conditionally stable coarse division, but changes the biological representation. The k=9 raw versus profile partitions agree poorly (ARI **0.140**); adding the type penalty changes the unguided profile k=9 partition substantially (ARI **0.166**). High guided stability is not independent evidence of biological classes. Singleton Jaccard is unassessed, rather than assigned a perfect score.

The historical single-monkey matrix covers **212**, not all 260 curated neurons; 48 extra annotation rows are explicitly recorded. Its raw k=2 split is 1/211. A profile-Spearman k=9 sensitivity run has mean subset ARI 0.903 and a singleton, while silhouette suggests k=7 within the tested range. Neither 7, 9, 20 nor 30 is accepted as the number of insular subtypes.

Priority: freeze/rebuild first; inspect distance-profile groups and tracing/outlier examples; compare with raw-length projection profiles; test whether groups add information beyond continuous soma position, animal/injection and axon completeness. Group deletion here reclusters the remaining animals; it is not prediction on an unseen animal.

## Measurement and inference limits

**Agent: Codex | Date: 2026-10-02 (Asia/Shanghai)**

- Current branch lengths are computed in NMT **voxel units**, with per-edge rounding, parent-voxel assignment and endpoint-based region filtering. The loader declares NMT physical micrometres before dividing by 250. Neither measurement should be called native physical axon length without calibrated native reconstruction and transform lineage.
- `Terminal_Count` and laterality counts in the population table count unique endpoint-containing **regions**, not individual endpoints or synapses. Compartment-specific axon measurement and edge-crossing allocation need an explicit future specification.
- The R path normalizes `log10(length+1)` strengths; historical shares such as “58% of axonal budget” need re-expression as transformed-strength shares or recomputation from raw axon lengths.
- Pooled neuron counts do not establish independent animal replication. Of staging dysgranular IDD5+IDM n=148 (85 L / 63 R), **139** are from 251637. Staging IAL is 33 L / 147 R and remains strongly imbalanced.
- Software topology checks preserve custom SWC types and dendrite-to-axon transitions; valid trees still require tracing/anatomical QC. Current sample processing skips failed neurons with a detailed error, so review exclusions before accepting a sample.
- VEN morphology, long-range class and molecular identity remain separate evidence. The Liu 2026 atlas leaves actual VEN subtype projection targets unresolved; clustering alone cannot establish VEN identity.
