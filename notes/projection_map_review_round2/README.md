## What was inspected

The second inspector imports no projection producer or endpoint detector. It independently reads every complete SWC, identifies non-root axon-labelled nodes that never occur in the original parent column, and assigns endpoints using a right-sided voxel-face search. Cropping cannot introduce an ending. It rebuilds each animal's endpoint counts and occupied-voxel fractions; two ends in one voxel count twice but contribute only one occupancy observation for that neuron.

All **452 NIfTIs** are checked for exact saved hashes, finite nonnegative values, reference shape/affine, coded transforms and millimetre units. Density multiplied by the determinant-derived voxel volume must recover count or length. Occupancy must lie in [0,1], share count support and never exceed count. Every group voxel must equal the mean of its declared contributing animal maps, with no missing-animal zero filling. Animal length integrals must agree with stored source summaries. The two historical endpoint partitions must be disjoint and have an exact 462-identity union. Each neuron retains its animal and ARM source assignment, and its end-branch length cannot exceed whole-axon length.

This is an additional, orthogonal inspection. The [earlier independent forward-edge/interval reader](../region_analysis_review_20261009/axon_end_branches_20261009/independent_end_branch_readback.json) checked every selected end-branch edge, every map voxel and all numeric/NA cells at six actual ARM levels. The current round does not claim to repeat that full branch-raster/region-cell analysis. No numerical alteration was needed following these checks.

## Visual method and paper references

The actual locally stored Zotero PDFs were rendered and inspected: **Gou et al. (2025), Figure 3, panels A/G/J/M, PDF page 7**, and **Gao et al. (2022), Figure 3, panels b/e/f/g, PDF page 7; caption on page 6**. Their contrasting treatment of trajectories, anatomical context and localized structures motivated a separate location display. Neither figure defines our SWC-leaf rule. Gou's arbors and Gao's terminal-arbor centroids involve additional processing; our markers must not be called their arbor centres. Verified local paths and PDF hashes are in the [prior Zotero methods record](../region_analysis_review_20261009/literature_method_update_20261009/gao_local_methods.json).

Gou et al., *Cell* 188:3806–3822.e1–e9, [10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.005), supports distinguishing axon targeting/arborization from whole trajectories. Gao et al., *Nature Neuroscience* 25:515–529, [10.1038/s41593-022-01041-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-022-01041-5), supports contextualized projection and terminal-arbor displays. The [Liu methods review](../region_analysis_review_20261009/literature_method_update_20261009/liu_local_methods.md) explains why an image-derived varicosity/arbor detector cannot be replaced by graph ends. These papers motivate the display; no mouse detection threshold, arbor classifier or statistical test is transferred.

**One dot represents one occupied endpoint voxel centre**, not one neuron, one ending or one arbor. All dots have the same area (9 points²), with a thin black rim and white halo for visibility. Colour encodes the existing equal-animal candidate-end density as log10(1+density), with one shared 99.5th-percentile upper display limit. The area is a readability choice with no quantitative meaning. Nearby markers can overlap at this display scale; use the NIfTI for quantitative inspection. Counts are not thinned, jittered, smoothed or moved to anatomical centroids.

The background is the symmetric NMT v2.1 **T1-weighted skull-stripped MRI**, not atlas labels or fMOST intensity. MRI contrast uses brain-mask percentiles 1–99.5. X56/Y200/Z87 are the existing matched voxel cuts; each is a single 0.25 mm voxel slice. There is no MIP, depth slab, threshold-based significance or spatial smoothing. Coordinates are labelled template axes/mm; physical laterality is not inferred from the historical affine alone. Source names are the complete official ARM level-6 names. The 52 atlas-background source cases remain in numerical ledgers/QC and are not presented as a named anatomical parcel. The new sheets visualize the original main 436-neuron partition; the 26 additional candidates remain separately audited and in the combined tables/end-branch outputs.

## Sorting and scientific limits

The [development archive readback](sorting/development_archive_readback.json) records superseded trial reports separately from final validation evidence.

Whole-axon maps average all selected neurons; endpoint and end-branch maps condition on end eligibility. Their units and denominators differ. Colours across these metric families must not be read as a common quantitative scale.

The sorting script rehashed **896 existing local artifacts** and mapped references before changing any location. All 172 untracked historical PNGs had saved-record references. Their paths, numerical NIfTIs, SWCs, original datasets and historical receipts therefore remain intact. The new current-output index separates procedures, inspection, figures and sorting records. Two temporary local paper-figure previews were moved into a narrowly ignored private archive with explicit old/new paths and exact-byte verification. No PDF or publisher figure is redistributed. Development/failed reproduction reports are archived separately, with paths/hashes in the archive readback; they are not validation evidence for the final procedure.

Software checks do not settle the unavailable authoritative fMOST-to-NMT transform/export-origin evidence, tracing completeness, fine anatomical membership or terminal-field identity. These remain unresolved, even when numerical checks pass. Endpoints and end-branches are candidate graph proxies; native template lengths are not calibrated tissue lengths. Group maps are descriptive, with no t-map, synapse claim or inference treating neurons from one animal as independent animals. Historical ARM key-range conflicts and missing/NA semantics remain explicit in the six-level tables.
