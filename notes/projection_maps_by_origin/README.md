## Operational evidence categories

All 462 partition into six non-overlapping categories: 212 ARM-insula with Henry coarse INS evidence; 89 ARM-insula without a Henry note; 35 Henry INS with conflicting right precentral operular assignment; 13 Henry INS with label 0; 74 other neighboring candidates; and 39 other label-0 candidates. **349 have INS support from ARM assignment, Henry evidence, or both; 113 remain other candidates.** This is an evidence union, not anatomical acceptance. Henry's coarse evidence concerns 260 neurons of 251637/936 and cannot independently confirm their fine ARM parcels. Label 0 is an unassigned atlas label, not a definitive native white-matter diagnosis. The historical source labels remain provenance; no neuron was automatically reclassified to force the total upward.

## Validation and limits

[Independent readback](independent_readback.json) checks the original 462 SWCs, unique soma/root identity, saved rounding policy, direct ARM source labels, all 75,625 original type-2 graph leaves, equal-animal denominators and all 30 new NIfTIs voxel by voxel. Each of the nine named-parcel projection maps also equals its established map exactly. Six focused regressions cover human evidence retention, unknown candidacy, coincident-soma counts and invalid source voxels. [Visual review](visual_review.json) records inspection of all 15 final map figures and the distribution figure. Long colourbar labels were corrected before final delivery; only this task's unpublished drafts were moved, with a [dependency/archive mapping](own_draft_archive_mapping.json).

Existing Gou/Gao/Liu literature-grounded marker conventions and graph-proxy limits are retained from the [second map review](../projection_map_review_round2/README.md) and [parsed Methods update](../region_analysis_review_20261009/scope_update_20261009.md). This follow-up changes grouping/display and records historical identities; it adds no new biological method. Graph leaves are candidate axon ends, not accepted terminal fields, boutons or synapses. Raw-to-NMT transform/export-origin evidence remains unavailable. Computational agreement and readable overlays do not establish anatomical acceptance. Unequal injection and reconstruction sampling remain evident; no statistical t-map was produced. CM032/CM033 work remains deferred.
