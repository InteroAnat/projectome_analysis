## Measurement and denominator contract

Use only the pinned NMT v2.1 symmetric ARM NIfTI/key and reference geometry. Each actual volume is a separate target scale, levels 1–6. Source headings retain the source **ARM level-6 full name, index and hemisphere**, while HenryINS/G/Candidate selection evidence remains independent metadata. No inferred parent labels, alternate atlas harmonization, cortical/subcortical homonym collapse, or sum across levels: the same endpoint/length is represented at every scale.

Lengths are **template-space axon-labelled segment length**, under the existing child-type-2 edge policy and exact voxel-face allocation. They are not native calibrated length, terminal-arbor length or synaptic strength. A selected graph lacking type-2 edges has zero under this label policy; that does not establish biological absence.

Endpoints are **non-root type-2 leaves in the complete graph**, not image-reviewed terminals, arbors, boutons or synapses. Count repeated endings separately; regional presence is binary per neuron. Multiple endpoints or occupied voxels in one actual target label count as one neuron with evidence. Summing voxel occupancy cannot estimate regional frequency.

The immutable main endpoint run has **436 selected/403 endpoint-computable** neurons; the existing preview run has **26 selected/26 endpoint-computable**. Thus the final integrated table has **462 selected/429 endpoint-computable** and 462 graphs for axon-length summaries. Eligibility means at least one candidate leaf anywhere, including outside the FOV. The 33 endpoint-unassessed neurons require NA in per-neuron endpoint matrices, not zero. An eligible neuron with all candidates outside a particular region has observed zero for that regional proxy and retains explicit outside evidence. Denominators are calculated within each animal/current-ARM source group; global cohort counts are not cell divisors.

For animal/source group `(a,s)`, selected count `N`, endpoint-eligible count `E`, per-neuron regional length `L`, candidate count `C` and presence `P=1(C>0)` at one actual level/label:

| Quantity | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Mean axon-labelled length | `sum_selected L / N` | Template mm per selected neuron |
| Mean candidate endpoint count | `sum_eligible C / E` | Candidate endings per eligible neuron |
| Regional candidate frequency | `sum_eligible P / E` | Fraction of eligible neurons, 0–1 |
| Optional regional density | Corresponding mean length/count divided by valid target volume | Per template mm³ per stated neuron denominator |
| Across-animal summary | Arithmetic mean of available animal/source means | Equal contributing-animal weights |

`E=0` gives unavailable endpoint means/frequency (NA), not zero. Missing animal/source groups are not zero-imputed. Contributing-animal counts are metric-specific. Pooled-neuron means and equal-animal means need distinct names if both appear. No t-test, p-value, FDR result or independent-neuron inference is implied.

## Associated clustering method review

The clustering runner uses one exclusive actual ARM level-6 positive mapped-target partition, retains absolute target hemispheres, and fits axon length and candidate endpoint profiles separately. Primary Hellinger dissimilarity is `||sqrt(p)-sqrt(q)|| / sqrt(2)`, where `p` is each neuron's within-mapped-target composition. Average linkage is explicitly declared. The log-relative sensitivity is `log1p(1000*p)` followed by row L2 normalization and Euclidean distance; the endpoint-presence sensitivity is binary Jaccard. These are different exploratory representations, not interchangeable measurements of synaptic strength. No cross-target z-score or mixed hierarchy feature duplication is appropriate here.

`independent_profile_distance_oracles.py` passed **four closed-form checks**: Hellinger orthogonal/half-mixture distances and invariance to total extent; Jaccard intersection/union distances independent of counts; refusal of zero/NA profiles; and refusal to silently substitute Ward linkage. The receipt only validates formulas/guards, not saved clustering outputs or biological classes.

Mapped-positive normalization conditions on the observed mapped fraction and removes absolute extent, potentially amplifying incompletely traced or low-coverage profiles. Keep total length/end count, mapped fraction, background and outside evidence alongside each assignment. Source ARM0 is not a clustering exclusion if its target profile is computable. Distinguish no eligible endpoints from eligible-but-no-positive-mapped-target evidence; neither becomes an artificial zero profile. Neuron-weighted clustering remains neuron-weighted even when equal-animal-cap subsampling is added as sensitivity. Repeated subset ARI/Jaccard, leave-one-animal-remaining-cohort agreement and silhouette are descriptive diagnostics, not held-out prediction, animal-independent inference, or biological cluster validation. The Henry-only sensitivity is one animal and must remain identified as such. Diagnostic choices of `k` or readable cluster size must not accept INS anatomy or cell types.

An actual input-schema blocker was reported before running: the exporter's out-of-FOV target rows have a blank/nullable `ARMIndex`, while the initial clustering loader cast the whole column directly to integer. Its coverage logic separately expected an outside sentinel. The loader must validate `TargetStatus`, preserve the actual nullable index and derive an internal sentinel only for known outside rows; positive features must be valid mapped labels. A successful exporter-status gate was also recommended before accepting artifact hashes. These guards were resolved before the real run; the completed saved-run check is recorded below.
