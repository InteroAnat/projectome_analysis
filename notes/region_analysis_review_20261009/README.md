## Applied repairs and readable terms

The active package now reconstructs absolute length from a lossless workbook, decodes serialized dictionaries explicitly, and rejects malformed values or unrecoverable split-only anatomy. Composite sample/neuron identity survives every matrix, hierarchy, terminal and outlier join. Contradictory, missing or duplicate identities fail explicitly. Standalone laterality reclassifies a justified absolute union with the resolved soma side, preserves unknown targets separately, and refuses output overwrite.

Cortical/subcortical homonyms retain their domains (`C_`/`S_`) before length aggregation; logarithms are taken after the final aggregation. Actual historical population pooling was observed for CM, R and RM. Numeric pineal/parainsula merging was not observed in these tables. New exports require coordinated downstream target definitions before an old R analysis can consume them.

Hierarchy loading preserves sparse declared levels, rejects conflicting ancestry, supports CSV/XLSX and incremental rollback, and uses the actual dual hierarchy through public APIs. Valid deep SWC trees use iterative branch construction with the original edge/order semantics. Plots accept current producer hierarchy columns and zero-valued data; lengths retain their declared source units. Harmonization preserves unrelated workbook cells, formulas, styles and metadata. Formula loss was demonstrated synthetically; none of the 12 inspected historical inputs contained formulas.

The [terminology guide](../../docs/region_analysis_terminology.md) defines each measure, denominator and script. Visible labels now distinguish retained reconstruction length, share of log-scaled length, distinct endpoint-target regions, candidate axon ends, endpoint density and neuron occupancy. Contralateral fraction (0–1), signed individual ipsi/contra balance (−1–1), and left-versus-right source-group contrasts have separate formulas and signs. Stored identifiers and legacy numerical definitions remain compatible.

F6 Rmd now matches the already established workflow in commit `d27c855b82a280989850d71f9f1eb994f70df58e`: presence and magnitude remain separate test families with strict `q < 0.05`. The saved historical 306-neuron statistics yield two presence and three magnitude display rows for three distinct targets. No p-values were recomputed and no fresh 353-neuron F6 result is implied. The [Methods update](methods_audit/methods_audit_updates_and_metric_contract_20261009.md) supersedes earlier receipts that described this synchronization as an open methodological choice or R as unavailable.

The [primary ARM figures](../../group_analysis/evolution_20261008/arm_mapping_20261009/README.md) use exact official ARM level-6 soma labels. One set of 12 sheets shows named parcels; the [52-case ledger](../../group_analysis/evolution_20261008/classification/background_neuron_audit_20261009/README.md) records unassigned locations. ## Scientific limits and reuse gates

- Legacy regional length includes retained reconstruction compartments, proximal whole-edge assignment, per-edge rounding and a region-wide graph-leaf gate. It is not an axon-only length or a validated terminal-field measurement. The separate descriptive maps use explicit type-2 edge/leaf definitions and template-space units; these methods must not be conflated.
- Atlas-labelled, Henry coarse human INS, reviewed provisional fine labels and coordinate candidates remain separate. Coordinate/root agreement and software tests do not establish registration, physical orientation or fine cytoarchitecture. The current export-origin alternative changes 227/1,912 labels; individual transform/landmark provenance remains unresolved.
- Historical units are missing from 74/100 unique Summary-containing workbooks, including the 353-neuron table. Missing receipts prevent unsupported unit conversion and scientific pooling. New mixed/partially specified unit pools fail explicitly.
- The legacy CT spans include 22 labels outside the exact SARM thalamus ancestry. All 13 observed CT neurons in the checked subset also contain true thalamic targets; wrong final classifications were not demonstrated for them. Exact-ancestry classification needs a versioned policy and anatomical adjudication, not silent relabelling.
- Old R consumers have target-namespace and cohort-alignment gates. The historical Chinese full tutorial also overloads `Laterality_Index` with incompatible formulas; it is unsuitable for reuse until its source contract is repaired. Overlapping L3/L6 features are not an exclusive anatomical budget; region-restricted strata are not animal-balanced strata. SampleID holdout is not automatically independent-animal holdout. BH correction does not resolve pseudoreplication or selection across strata.
- Graph ends are candidates, not verified terminal arbors, boutons or synapses. Native tissue, truncation and terminal-arbor review remain necessary. Descriptive maps across eight animals do not supply animal-level inference for every source stratum; Henry HumanINS has one animal. No inferential t-map is produced.
- CM032's actual signed contrast is numerically reproduced with explicit SPM estimation-mask coverage. This does not accept subject registration. CM033's recorded orientation rejection and absent accepted NMT model prevent spatial integration. Joint regional values remain provisional; no anatomical, causal or monosynaptic correspondence is claimed.

The [complete monkey/dataset inventory and mapping report](../../group_analysis/evolution_20261008/reports/evolution_status_20261009.md) retains all 47 portal sample IDs plus two tracker-only IDs, unknown versus locally unavailable 5 µm sources, the actual 251637 screen and the 462-neuron descriptive map selection. Original raw data, human annotations, canonical cohorts and unrelated work remain protected.
